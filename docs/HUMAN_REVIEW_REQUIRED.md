# Human Review Required

> **SUPERSEDED (2026-11-15):** this list was written for the former local synthetic MVP and its excluded capabilities. It is not the active blocker list. See [API_CONTRACT_BLOCKERS_AND_DECISIONS.md](../API_CONTRACT_BLOCKERS_AND_DECISIONS.md) for current actual-MVP decisions; [API_CONTRACT.md](../API_CONTRACT.md) is authoritative.

The decisions below are retained as historical review questions only. Their former defaults to keep authentication, actual data, history, or trends unavailable are no longer active product policy.

| Decision IDs | Exact question requiring human review | Safe default until approved |
|---|---|---|
| D-01, D-02, D-22 | Before any shared, networked, or non-synthetic use, is the local no-authentication exception acceptable, and which identity provider, session lifecycle, roles, profile assignment, authorization checks, and real-person privacy policy will apply? | Keep the service loopback-only and synthetic-only. Do not add authentication claims, shared access, or real-person data. |
| D-07, D-08 | Which check-in scales and symptom vocabulary, if any, are approved; may free text be collected; who can access it; and what are the retention and shared-view rules? | No check-in or symptom routes, forms, private notes, ratings, or diagnosis claims. |
| D-09, D-15 | What local support-request schema and lifecycle are approved; what exact backend evidence constitutes acknowledgement; who may acknowledge/resolve; and is any external delivery provider in scope? | No support or alert capability, storage, queue, transmission, notification, acknowledgement, or claim of receipt. |
| D-10, D-11 | What exact assessment request/response and snapshot lifecycle are approved, including the coverage enum and aggregation independent of status; which complete, versioned rules and tests are authorized; and how is an explicit immediate-help event represented? | No assessment endpoint or result, no `insufficient_data`/`not_configured` placeholder, no executable rule or numerical threshold, and no numerical production of `urgent`. |
| D-12 | What baseline statistic, precision, comparability and freshness policy, activation/replacement lifecycle, and immutable snapshot reference are approved? | No baseline operation, calculation, storage, or baseline-dependent assessment. |
| D-13 | What exact trend route, filters, aggregation and rounding, blood-pressure grouping, eligible-record rules, pagination, empty-day behavior, and count definitions are approved? | No trend endpoint or aggregation. Do not interpret the raw record lists as trends. |
| D-16 | What exercise plan/log fields, activity and adherence vocabulary, lifecycle, permissions, and shared-record identity are approved? | No exercise plan, log, adherence claim, or body-measurement inference. |
| D-17 | Is any radiation quantity approved, and if so what exact quantity semantics, unit representation and precision, interval boundaries/overlap, selected-stream identity, provenance, and source are authorized? Is any mission-specific threshold approved by an appropriate owner? | No radiation record, dose field, unit, conversion, interval, or threshold. Do not treat candidate `personal_dose_equivalent_hp10` or `mSv` names in historical proposals as adopted. |
| D-18 | Which events and privacy projection may appear in a timeline; what timestamp, ordering, range, and pagination apply; and what convergence window, boundaries, and deduplication rules are approved? | No timeline, convergence marker, or cross-domain aggregation. Never describe temporal co-occurrence as causation. |
| D-20 | For any new resource family, who assigns and reviews provenance/quality, which exact values and field semantics are approved, and how are stale, suspect, invalid, missing, or incomplete records handled? | Do not add unapproved fields or enums; keep the resource unavailable. Missing, stale, suspect, or incomplete inputs must not be coerced to zero or a favorable status. |
| D-21 | What correction, deletion, export, retention, and audit policy applies beyond the local database's own lifetime? | Keep the included API append-only; do not promise retention, deletion, or export behavior. |

The six canonical domain identifiers remain exactly `cardiovascular`, `sleep_fatigue`, `psychological`, `musculoskeletal`, `immune`, and `radiation`; no review here authorizes aliases. Assessment status and coverage remain separate dimensions. No clinical or NASA threshold is authorized by this document.
